About

Dominique Deamues, founder and principal engineer

Aurum Equity LLC is a woman-owned small business in Birmingham, Alabama. Dominique is a senior software engineer and Certified ScrumMaster who leads every engagement directly.

Dominique has built and modernized enterprise web platforms, analytics systems, and workflow applications. Aurum Equity applies that experience to federal work, with a focus on compliance-ready modernization: updating legacy systems and delivering them with the documentation reviewers expect.

Aurum Equity is a new company and has not yet performed work under its own contracts. Larger efforts can be supported by vetted specialist partners as needed.
