How Aurum plugs in

Two ways to engage

Defined work packages

Aurum takes a bounded piece of the statement of work, such as a modernization task, an integration, or a documentation deliverable, and owns it through acceptance.

Embedded engineers

A senior engineer joins your team and works inside your process, tools, and cadence. This is the Technical Surge Support offer.

What primes get

Less overhead, clear accountability

  • Documentation written during delivery, not after
  • Small-business subcontracting participation toward your plan goals
