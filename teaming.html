Small-business participation

Aurum Equity is a small business, so subcontract dollars may count toward your small-business subcontracting goals. Aurum is woman-owned and its SBA WOSB certification is pending. Please do not count Aurum toward WOSB goals until certification is confirmed.

Aurum fits software development, application modernization, systems integration, AI and workflow automation, records retention and lifecycle work, technical surge support, and defined work packages within larger federal programs. Aurum can work as an independent delivery partner or integrate with an established program team.

Based in Birmingham, Alabama, near the Huntsville and Redstone Arsenal federal corridor. Aurum is building relationships in the region and is available to support programs in the Southeast and remotely.
