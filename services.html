Services

Three starter offers with defined scope

Each offer is small enough to start quickly and specific enough to evaluate. Pricing is shared on a call.

Fixed price2 to 3 weeks

Legacy Application Assessment

A focused review of one legacy application that ends with a plan, not a pile of notes.

Scope

  • Current-state review of architecture, code, data, and hosting
  • Dependency, integration, and technical debt inventory
  • Security and maintainability risk findings
  • Interviews with the people who run and support the application

Deliverables

  • Current-state summary
  • Risk findings ranked by impact
  • Modernization roadmap with recommended sequence
  • Readout session with your team

Typical timeline

Two to three weeks from access to final readout. The timeline depends on application size and how quickly access is provided.

Fixed price

ATO and Security Documentation Readiness Review

A gap analysis of your system against the documentation that authorization requires. Aurum does not grant or guarantee an ATO. The authorizing official decides. This review shows your team where the documentation stands and what to close first.

Scope

  • Review of existing system documentation and artifacts
  • Comparison against authorization documentation needs for the target framework
  • Identification of missing, outdated, or inconsistent material
  • Prioritized remediation plan

Deliverables

  • Gap analysis report
  • Prioritized remediation list
  • Documentation outline for the missing items
  • Readout session with your team

Typical timeline

The timeline is set once the system and target framework are known. A call is the fastest way to confirm it.

Time and materials

Technical Surge Support

A senior engineer embedded with your program team to add capacity where it is needed.

Scope

  • Hands-on software development and defect remediation
  • Integration, API, and workflow automation work
  • Release and deployment support
  • Runbooks and knowledge transfer to your staff

Deliverables

  • Working software delivered through your existing process
  • Documentation for the work performed
  • Regular status updates in your preferred format

Typical timeline

Engagements start after a short ramp-up and run for the term your program needs. Larger needs may be supported by vetted specialist partners as needed.
