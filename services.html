01 · Fixed price

Legacy Application Assessment

You know the system is aging. You need to know what it will take to fix, in what order, and what happens if you wait.

Typical timeline: A short, defined engagement. The schedule is set in the written scope once access to the system is confirmed.

Request this assessment

Scope

  • Current-state review of architecture, code, data, and hosting
  • Dependency, integration, and technical debt inventory
  • Security and maintainability risk findings
  • Interviews with the people who run and support the application

You receive

  • Current-state summary
  • Risk findings ranked by impact
  • Modernization roadmap with recommended sequence
  • Readout session with your team

Good fit if

  • Original developers are gone
  • Releases are slow or risky
  • Budget planning needs a real estimate
02 · Fixed price

ATO and Security Documentation Readiness Review

Authorization delays usually come from missing or outdated documentation. This review finds those gaps early and shows what to close first. Aurum does not grant or guarantee an ATO. The authorizing official decides.

Typical timeline: Set once the system and target framework are known. A call is the fastest way to confirm it.

Request this review

Scope

  • Review of existing system documentation and artifacts
  • Comparison against authorization documentation needs for the target framework
  • Identification of missing, outdated, or inconsistent material
  • Prioritized remediation plan

You receive

  • Gap analysis report
  • Prioritized remediation list
  • Documentation outline for the missing items
  • Readout session with your team

Good fit if

  • An authorization date is coming
  • System changes outpaced the docs
  • Your team lacks time to prepare
03 · Time and materials

Technical Surge Support

A senior engineer who joins your team quickly, works inside your process, and leaves the codebase better documented than it was found.

Typical timeline: Starts after a short ramp-up and runs for the term your program needs. Larger needs may be supported by vetted specialist partners as needed.

Request surge support

Scope

  • Hands-on software development and defect remediation
  • Integration, API, and workflow automation work
  • Release and deployment support
  • Runbooks and knowledge transfer to your staff

You receive

  • Working software delivered through your existing process
  • Documentation for the work performed
  • Regular status updates in your preferred format

Good fit if

  • A deadline is at risk
  • A key engineer just left
  • Backlog keeps growing
