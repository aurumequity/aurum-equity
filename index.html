How an engagement runs

Clear scope before any work begins.

  1. Step 1

    Capability call

    A short conversation about your system, your timeline, and how you plan to buy.

  2. Step 2

    Written scope

    A short statement of work with deliverables, schedule, and a fixed price or hourly rate.

  3. Step 3

    Delivery

    Regular status updates and working sessions with your team. No surprises.

  4. Step 4

    Handoff

    Documentation, runbooks, and a walkthrough so your team owns the result.
